# Adidas_Sales_Dashboard
This project focuses on analyzing Adidas sales data to understand sales performance across different products, regions, retailers, and sales methods. The analysis was carried out in Microsoft Excel using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, and other visualization tools to identify important trends and business insights.

📊 Dataset Summary
Total Records: 10,000 orders
Total Sales: ₹1.76 Crore
Total Units Sold: 3,31,328
Average Sales per Order: ₹1,761
Average Price per Unit: ₹52.93
Total Products: 16
Total Retailers: 8
Total States: 20
Years Covered: 2022–2024

💡 Key Insights
Southeast Region recorded the highest sales.
J.D. Sports was the top retailer by sales.
Accessories generated the highest sales among products.
Online was the most successful sales method.
2023 recorded the highest total sales among the three years.
October was the highest-sales month.
Accessories also recorded the highest number of units sold.
The dataset contains 16 products sold through 8 retailers across 20 states.
Online sales generated more revenue than Outlet and In-Store sales.
